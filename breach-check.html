Has this email shown up in a data breach?

Enter an email to see which known breaches it appeared in and what categories of data were exposed. We never display, store, or look up actual passwords — for anyone's account, including your own.

Enter an email to check for exposure.
Why no passwords, ever. Showing a breached password lets anyone try it against other accounts — that's account takeover, not protection. This tool tells you where you were exposed and what kind of data leaked, which is what you need to actually respond: change that password and turn on 2FA.
Data source note. This demo uses simulated results. Wire up runBreachCheck() in js/main.js to a backend endpoint that queries a legitimate breach-directory API (e.g. Have I Been Pwned) with a server-side key, respecting its terms of use.
